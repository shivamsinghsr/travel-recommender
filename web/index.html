        How it works

        New here? Your picks come from the kinds of places you say you enjoy, matched against each destination's type, region and season, plus how well other travellers rate it.

        As you rate places, collaborative filtering gets a growing share of the say: places rated highly by travellers whose ratings resemble yours. How big a share is tuned nightly on held-out trips.

        Filters come first. Pick a month and you only see places that are in season then. Places you've rated never come back as suggestions.

        Destinations are real. Traveller ratings are simulated for this demo.
